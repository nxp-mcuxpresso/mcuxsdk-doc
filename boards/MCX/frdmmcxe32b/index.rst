FRDM-MCXE32B
####################

Overview
********

FRDM-MCXE32B is a development board for early access evaluation of MCX E32x MCUs.

MCU device and part on board is shown below:

 - Device: MCXE32B
 - PartNumber: MCXE32BMPB
