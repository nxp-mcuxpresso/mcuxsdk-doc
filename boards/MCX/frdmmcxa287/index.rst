FRDM-MCXA287
####################

Overview
********

FRDM-MCXA287 is a compact development board for rapid prototyping of MCX A28X MCUs.

MCU device and part on board is shown below:

 - Device: MCXA287
 - PartNumber: MCXA287VPN
